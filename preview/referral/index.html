Terms & conditions

1. Eligibility

Participants must be 18 or older, with a valid email address and ID proof.

2. Referral validity

The referred client must sign a contract for a project above the minimum qualifying size of ₹5,00,000.

3. Reward distribution

Rewards are paid in line with the client’s payment phases.

4. Payment method

Rewards are paid by bank transfer or in cash.

5. One-time use

A reward applies once per project and can’t be combined with other discounts or promotions.

6. Fraud prevention

Designo may investigate suspicious, fraudulent or abusive activity and may withhold or cancel rewards, end a participant’s involvement or pursue legal remedies. Participants agree to cooperate with any investigation.

7. Changes to the programme

Designo may modify, suspend or end the programme at any time without notice, and may offer a grace period to redeem outstanding rewards.

8. Dispute resolution

Disputes go first to Designo’s designated representative. If unresolved, both parties use a mutually agreed mediator and bear their own costs unless agreed otherwise.

9. Privacy and data protection

Personal information is used only to run the programme, in line with our Privacy Policy. You can ask to access, update or delete it, subject to legal or contractual obligations.
