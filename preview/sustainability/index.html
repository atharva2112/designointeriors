Designo EcoVisions · ISO 14001

A greener
tomorrow

Sustainability runs through how we specify, source and build. Every client who works with us contributes to a cleaner, safer environment as well as a better space.

01 — Why it matters

Better for people and place

Environmental preservation

Materials and methods that use fewer finite resources, create less waste and do less harm to habitats.

Healthier indoor air

Low-VOC materials reduce respiratory irritation, allergies and headaches for the people inside.

Resource conservation

Designs that save water and energy every day the space is in use.

Lower carbon footprint

Energy-efficient appliances, local sourcing and natural light all cut emissions.

Long-term savings

Efficient systems and durable materials lower running and maintenance costs.

Well-being

Natural elements and healthy materials make spaces more comfortable to live and work in.

02 — Our initiatives

What we do on every project

  1. 01

    Low-VOC paints and finishes

    Finishes that don’t release harmful chemicals into the air, safer for occupants, our team and the planet.

  2. 02

    Energy-efficient lighting and appliances

    Fittings that use less electricity and water, plus natural light and ventilation wherever possible to reduce cooling loads.

  3. 03

    Local sourcing

    We work with local artisans and suppliers, which cuts transport emissions.

  4. 04

    Waste reduction

    Usable materials from demolition are donated to local charities and recycling centres.

  5. 05

    Eco-tech integration

    Smart lighting, renewable energy and efficient appliances that save or generate electricity.

  6. 06

    Water conservation

    Water-saving fixtures, rainwater harvesting and water management systems.

03 — What you gain

Your benefits

01

Lower energy costs

Renewable sources, low-energy appliances and durable materials reduce utility and repair bills.

02

Higher property value

Green buildings tend to command better occupancy, rents and resale values.

03

Tax incentives

Depending on the project, green building measures such as rooftop solar can qualify for subsidies or rebates.

04

Built for Mumbai’s climate

Comfortable, resilient spaces in a hot, humid city, with less heat stress and lower emissions.
