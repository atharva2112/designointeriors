Vastu Shastra

Planned
for balance

Vastu Shastra is the traditional Indian system of architecture. Its principles cover layout, measurement, orientation and spatial geometry, with the aim of bringing a building into harmony with nature.

The arrangement of an interior affects the people who use it. In dense city buildings a fully Vastu-planned layout isn’t always possible, so careful choices of furniture, colour and accessories can bring a space into compliance.
