About Designo

Since 1991

Designo Interiors is a corporate interior design and turnkey project firm based in Mumbai. We plan, design and build workplaces, retail, hospitality and residential interiors, and manage every trade on site under one contract.

01 — How we work

Our style

Contemporary design that respects the building it sits in. We keep original features and character where they matter, and shape each space around how the client works.

Our approach

A personal service from brief to handover. We listen, advise and develop a concept that meets your expectations and your budget, then manage every detail of delivery.

Our network

Three decades of suppliers, contractors, craftsmen and artisans who share our standards, with materials and furniture sourced from local and international brands.

04 — Certifications

Certified three times over

Designo is one of very few interior design firms in India certified for quality, environmental, and occupational health and safety management.

ISO 9001Quality Management System

The most widely recognised quality standard in the world. It sets out how we plan, run and continually improve project delivery, so the result is consistent from one site to the next.

ISO 14001Environmental Management System

A framework for identifying and controlling the environmental impact of our work. It guides how we cut waste, conserve resources and prevent pollution, and how we choose eco-friendly, energy-efficient materials.

ISO 45001Occupational Health & Safety System

The global standard for occupational health and safety. It governs how we manage risk on site to protect employees, contractors and clients, and the hygiene we keep in occupied buildings.
